Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guidance for common library enquiries.

The library primarily serves the Lira University community. Ask library staff about visitor access and any applicable requirements.

Visit the library service desk and follow the current borrowing and registration procedures.

Ask library staff which electronic databases, journals and e-books are currently available and how to access them.

Speak to library staff for guidance on finding information, using academic sources and referencing.

Opening hours may change during semesters, holidays and university activities. Confirm the current schedule directly with the library.

Report the loss to library staff promptly and follow the official library replacement or compensation procedure.

For official policies, current opening hours and specific account enquiries, please contact the University Library directly.
